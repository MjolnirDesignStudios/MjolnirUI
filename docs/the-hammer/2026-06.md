## Section 3 — The story (the one thing)

> **Pick one moment that surprised you during the build.** Below are three
> candidates from what actually happened — pick the one that has the most
> juice, or replace with something that happens between now and 6/24.

### Candidate A — "I shipped 4 weeks of charts in 2 nights"

The June roadmap had 4 Friday slots — week 1 was BarChart/LineChart/DonutChart,
then DataTable / Heatmap / GSAP spread across the rest. On the evening of
June 11 I sat down to do "tonight's tranche," ended up shipping all 6 charts,
then immediately rolled into DataTable + Heatmap + 3 more. Two commits, 11
components, ~1,100 insertions.

The lesson: my weekly cadence was overcautious. The shared `chartTheme.ts`
file meant each subsequent chart cost 20 minutes, not 60. Brand evolution
became a one-file change. **Tooling that compounds is what makes solo
shipping sustainable.**

### Candidate B — "The dangerous-account-linking flag I shipped on launch day"

The MDS cross-grant means I pre-create a `next_auth.users` row by Stripe
customer email when someone subscribes to MDS. For NextAuth to then link
a Google/GitHub OAuth sign-in to that pre-existing row, you have to set
`allowDangerousEmailAccountLinking: true`. The flag name reads scary.

I shipped it anyway on launch day because the threat model didn't apply:
the source of every pre-created row is a Stripe customer record — Stripe
verifies email ownership at payment time, so the email-takeover attack
vector is closed. **Defaults exist for the median case; understand the
specific case before respecting or overriding them.**
